Dataflow Gen2 is the new generation of dataflows. The new generation of dataflows resides alongside the Power BI Dataflow (Gen1) and brings new features and improved experiences.

Join us in this Fabric Espresso episode as Miguel and Estera discuss Dataflows Gen2 and provide a comparison between Dataflow Gen1 and Dataflow Gen2.
