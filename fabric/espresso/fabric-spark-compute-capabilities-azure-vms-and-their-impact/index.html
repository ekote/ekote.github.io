Fabric Spark Compute Capabilities - Azure VM's and their impact on performance

Episode 44 of Fabric Espresso, hosted by Dr. Estera Kot.

About this episode

Text from the YouTube description.

In this episode, Guy and Estera discuss how Fabric Spark, a distributed data processing platform, leverages Azure VM's to achieve high performance and scalability. They compare Fabric Spark with Synapse Spark, and highlight the differences in terms of architecture, features, and pricing.

Read more: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/data-engineering/spark-compute
