📚👨‍💻 Data Engineer's Perspective: Loading Data into Fabric - Comprehensive Guide 🚀📈

In this enlightening video, we delve into the world of data engineering and explore how to effectively load data into Fabric, the powerful all-in-one analytics solution for enterprises that covers everything from data movement to data science, Real-Time Analytics, and business intelligence.

We're thrilled to welcome a special guest, Ted Vilutis, an experienced product manager, to our Data Engineering and Data Science series hosted by Estera Kot. Together, they will illuminate the subtleties of various data loading methods and provide their expert insights.

🔑 Key Points:

👉 Upload CSV: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/data-engineering/get-started-csv-upload

👉 Create your first dataflow: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/data-factory/create-first-dataflow-gen2

👉 Microsoft Fabric decision guide: copy activity, dataflow, or Spark: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/get-started/decision-guide-pipeline-dataflow-spark

👉 Shortcuts to Other Storage Locations: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/onelake/onelake-shortcuts
