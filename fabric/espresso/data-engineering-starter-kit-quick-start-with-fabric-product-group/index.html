🌐 Welcome back to the Fabric Espresso series! Today's episode is a goldmine for data engineers and enthusiasts diving into the Microsoft Fabric platform.

🔍 Join us as we welcome Avinanda (Avi) Chattapadday, Product Manager from the Microsoft Fabric Data Engineering team. Avi is instrumental in driving the Lakehouse experience and its integration across the Fabric platform. In this episode, she's here to guide us through a hands-on demo of two incredible samples available for data engineers.

🚀 What's in Store?

No-Hassle Provisioning: Learn how, with just a subscription or trial subscription, you can effortlessly click on samples and choose your favorite one. Microsoft Fabric takes care of provisioning the necessary resources, including a fully equipped lakehouse and notebooks.

Step-by-Step Demo: Avi walks us through the most advanced and complex samples for data engineering. These samples are designed to get you coding and using Spark immediately, leveraging the full features of Spark and Lakehouse.

Practical Applications: Discover how these samples allow you to load data, conduct data analysis, and install necessary libraries. It's the perfect toolkit for both junior and senior data engineers.

👩‍💻 Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned professional, this episode is a fantastic starting point to familiarize yourself with Microsoft Fabric. Avi's step-by-step guide ensures that you'll be ready to tackle data engineering tasks with ease and just improve your overall understanding of Spark.

💡 Use this episode as a tutorial or a first step in your Microsoft Fabric journey. By the end of it, you'll be ready to explore, experiment, and excel in the world of data engineering.
