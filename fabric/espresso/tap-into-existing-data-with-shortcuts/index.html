About this episode

Text from the YouTube description.

Welcome to our Fabric Espresso series! In this video, Stijn and Filip demonstrate how you can leverage OneLake shortcuts to query the data without copying it. Besides explaining what shortcuts are and how to create them, they explain which of your actions on the shortcut reflect to the data in the original storage.

OneLake, the OneDrive for data: https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/onelake/onelake-overview
OneLake shortcuts: https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/onelake/onelake-shortcuts
How do shortcuts handle deletions?: https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/onelake/onelake-shortcuts?branch=main#how-do-shortcuts-handle-deletions
