Fabric Espresso

The Microsoft Fabric video series by Dr. Estera Kot

Fabric Espresso is a technical video series on Microsoft Fabric. Dr. Estera Kot created the series and hosts it. Most episodes feature guests from the Microsoft Fabric product group, the engineers and product managers who build the features under discussion. The YouTube descriptions name Dr. Kot as host or speaker on 53 of the 89 episodes. Product group colleagues host others, including the Warehouse series.

Episodes cover Spark in Fabric, the Native Execution Engine, Data Engineering, Data Science, Warehouse, Data Factory, Real-Time Intelligence, and governance. The 89 episodes run about 22 hours in total. Episodes were published between June 2023 and December 2025.

The full series is on YouTube: Fabric Espresso playlist.

Episodes are grouped by topic and numbered in publication order. Each episode page carries the YouTube description, the chapter list where YouTube has one, and a link to the video.
