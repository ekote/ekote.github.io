Fabric Espresso Presents: " Granular Access Control for Warehouse "

Do you want to know how to only give access to single tables/stored procedures/ views / functions in Fabric Warehouse? Then check out this video on Granular Access control for warehouse! In this episode Filip & Stijn talk about the two levels of permissions the workspace permissions and the SQL Permission and how to combine these two to grant granular object access in Fabric Warehouse 📊
